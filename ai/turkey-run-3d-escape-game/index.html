Turkey Run - A Simple 3D Escape Web Game

Last updated on November 23, 2026 pm

Overview

Ahead of Thanksgiving, I had AI generate a lighthearted little 3D web mini-game: Turkey Run — NO ROAST FOR ME!

It is a straightforward, simple escape demo built with Three.js. You play as a turkey trying to avoid becoming dinner—dodge the patrolling farmer and his dog, hide in bushes, and reach the open gate to escape.

  • Play Demo: Turkey Run

Simple Gameplay

There is no complex game design here—just a pure, casual stealth loop:

  • Move: W A S D or Arrow keys (or on-screen joystick on mobile).
  • Sprint: Hold Shift. Faster, but drains stamina and makes noise.
  • Hide: Hold Space or H near bushes to stay out of sight.
  • Goal: Reach the yard gate without getting caught.

A fun experiment showing how modern AI can quickly put together low-poly 3D assets, basic patrol logic, and web controls into a playable mini-game.
