Evolving the 3D Rainy Night Convenience Store | DevLog & AI Iterations

Last updated on November 22, 2026 am

Overview

Following our initial three-model side-by-side benchmark, the 3D rainy night convenience store diorama stood out as an engaging canvas for creative coding.

This devlog tracks the step-by-step evolution of the scene, its technical highlights, and key takeaways from incremental AI pair programming in Three.js.

Lastest demo
Rainy Night Convenience Store

Update

  • 2026-11-20: Major update introducing full dynamic environment controls, scene polish, interactive elements, and analytics:
    • Connected the two road segments, retained only the south crosswalk, and corrected its orientation.
    • Adjusted camera zoom limits to allow closer inspection of details.
    • Added switching across Morning / Noon / Evening / Midnight combined with Rain / Cloudy / Clear skies; dynamically coordinates skybox, environment lighting, exposure, streetlamps, neon signs, interior lights, and puddle reflections.
    • Added a cat that moves based on the time of day, taking a nap around noon.
    • Clickable rooftop “BLOG” lightbox with an animated racing light border, integrated with Google Analytics (GA) and Microsoft Clarity.
    • Rainy Night Convenience Store (Updated with billboard, weather, cat & analytics)
  • 2026-11-19: Updated the DeepSeek version to include two characters outside the convenience store. Rainy Night Convenience Store(Updated with Characters)
